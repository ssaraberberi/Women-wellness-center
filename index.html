01 — The idea

More than a workout.

DRITA is a small studio built on one idea — that movement should be something you look forward to, not something you get through.

Reformer, barre, yoga and recovery live under one roof, so strength, stillness and care stop competing for space in your week. You come after work. You leave lighter. You come back on Thursday.

Eight women to a class. Instructors who know your name and your shoulders. Warm light, textured walls, and a long bench by the window for the coffee afterwards.

02 — The signature

Stronger, with intention.

Six reformers, eight women, forty-five minutes. Slow, controlled, precisely cued — the kind of strength that shows up in your posture before it shows up in the mirror.

Strength.

Progressive spring resistance that builds real capacity — without ever pounding your joints.

Control.

Every repetition is slow enough that you feel exactly which muscle is doing the work.

Posture.

Deep core and back work that undoes eight hours at a desk, one class at a time.

Confidence.

Beginner-friendly foundations. You'll never be the person in the room who doesn't know what's happening.

Movement.

Mobility you keep — in the way you walk, lift, sit and carry yourself through Tirana.

  • Small groupsMaximum eight women, six reformers, one instructor who watches all of you.
  • Beginner-friendlyA free 30-minute foundations session before your first group class.
  • Hands-onPersonal corrections in every single class. That's the whole point.

06 — The space

Sheshi Wilson, Tiranë.

Top floor of a 1930s building a minute from the square — high ceilings, deep windows and the kind of afternoon light you can't install. Lime-plaster walls, oak, linen, and a shower room stocked so you can come straight from the office and leave straight for dinner.
